# Privacy Policy for My Tracker

_Last updated: 2026-05-14_

This Privacy Policy explains how the My Tracker mobile application (“App”, “we”, “us”, “our”) processes personal data and protects the privacy of users (“you”, “user”).

The App is designed as an offline habit and mood tracker with a calendar, statistics, and personal journal. All data is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers or third parties, unless you explicitly export and share it yourself.[^local]

> **Important:** This document is provided for informational purposes only and does not constitute legal advice. For full legal compliance in your specific situation, please consult a qualified lawyer.

[^local]: The App uses local storage (e.g., SQLite) on the device; there is no account system, cloud sync, or analytics SDKs embedded in the App.

---

## 1. Data Controller

Since the App does not use a backend and processes data only locally on your device, in most cases **you** are the primary party controlling your data.

For purposes of EU General Data Protection Regulation (“GDPR”), California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA (“CCPA/CPRA”), and Federal Law of the Russian Federation No. 152-FZ “On Personal Data”, the developer of the App may act as a data controller only to the very limited extent that any personal data is processed outside of the App (for example, when you contact support by email).[^rights]

## 3. Categories of Data We Process

### 3.1. Data stored locally on your device (by the App)

The App may store the following categories of information **only on your device**:

- **Habit data**  
  Names of habits, their status per day (completed / not completed), streaks, counters.

- **Mood and journal entries**  
  Selected mood/emotions, free-form text notes you write in the journal.

- **Statistics and derived data**  
  Aggregated statistics (streaks, totals, charts and heatmaps) generated from your input.

- **App settings**  
  UI theme (light/dark), notification preferences, language, and other configuration options.

This data is **not automatically transmitted** to us or to third parties. You may choose to export data (for example, to a file) and share it manually; in that case, you control where and how the export is stored.

### 3.2. Data we may receive when you contact us

If you contact us (e.g., by email or via issue tracker), we may process:

- Your name or nickname;
- Email address and message content;
- Any diagnostic information you choose to send (screenshots, logs, etc.).

This data is used only to respond to your request and improve the App.

## 6. Data Storage and Security

### 6.1. Local storage on your device

- All App data (habits, statistics, notes, moods, settings) is stored locally on your device in a local database or similar storage.
- You are responsible for securing access to your device (e.g., using a passcode, Touch ID, Face ID).

### 6.2. Security measures

We aim to follow reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect any personal data that we might process outside the App (for example, support emails), including:

- Use of secure communication channels (HTTPS, TLS) when applicable;
- Restricting access to such data to authorized persons only;
- Retaining only the minimum amount of data necessary and for as short a period as possible.[web:170]

However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. You should be aware of the risks inherent in email and other Internet communications.

### 7.1. Data on your device

## 8. Your Rights Under GDPR (EU/EEA)

If you are located in the European Union, the European Economic Area (EEA), or other regions with similar data protection laws, you may have the following rights regarding personal data we process as a controller:

- **Right to be informed** – To know what data we collect and how we use it.[web:165][web:174]  
- **Right of access** – To request a copy of your personal data we hold.  
- **Right to rectification** – To correct inaccurate or incomplete data.  
- **Right to erasure (“right to be forgotten”)** – To request deletion of your personal data where applicable.  
- **Right to restriction of processing** – To request that we limit how we process your data.  
- **Right to data portability** – To request your data in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format, where technically feasible.[web:168]  
- **Right to object** – To object to certain types of processing.  
- **Right not to be subject to automated decision-making** – We do not perform automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the contact details in Section 1. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

> **Note:** For data stored only on your device and not transmitted to us, you can exercise most of these rights directly by deleting or editing data within the App or uninstalling it.
